Drone Swarm Optimisation

How do multiple drones coordinate without central control?

Solved instability and inefficient coordination in drone swarms by applying Particle Swarm Optimisation to their behavioural parameters. The system enables drones to self-organise, improving formation cohesion and coverage without central control.

Swarm intelligence Autonomous systems Decentralised coordination

Overview

Solved instability and inefficient coordination in drone swarms by applying Particle Swarm Optimisation to their behavioural parameters. The system enables drones to self-organise, improving formation cohesion and coverage without central control.

Result

Demonstrated that coordinated swarm behaviour can arise without central control through simple local interactions.

Impact

  • Distributed decisions shaping global outcomes
  • Emergent dynamic roles within the swarm
  • Continuous adaptation to environmental changes
